Basket is a shopping app. It collects product and price data from more than 30,000 online shops. I was responsible for the data collection platform, the AI features and the infrastructure on AWS and GCP.

  • 85% → 95%+success rate, old to new platform
  • 25 s → 3 saverage time per page

  • In March 2026 I replaced the old platform with a new version that I designed and built in approximately two weeks. AI agents wrote the code to my design and under my review.

How I work

  • I measure before I spend. At Basket, the data showed that less than 5% of failures were blocks. So the money went to extraction and not to more expensive proxies.
  • A fetch is successful only when the extractor gets the data: title, price and currency. The HTTP status is not enough.
  • A backup is not done until a restore test passes on a separate system.
  • All infrastructure is in Terraform, and alerts are code too.
  • AI agents write most of my code. I own the design and I review every change.
